Frequently asked questions

How do I book an appointment +

Open the Appointments page, enter your details, choose a doctor, date and time, then confirm the demo booking. It will be saved in this browser only.

Are these real doctors +

No. Doctor names, profiles, ratings and availability are fictional sample content for an educational frontend project. This site does not provide medical services.

Can I cancel a demo appointment +

Yes. Open the Appointments page to create demo bookings. You can review and remove saved demo appointments from the Health Dashboard.

Is my health information stored securely +

This frontend demo stores booking and checklist examples in your browser's local storage. Do not enter real medical, financial, or sensitive personal information.

Is this a real healthcare service +

No. MediConnect is a student demonstration project. It is not connected to clinics, medical records, or emergency services. For medical advice, contact a qualified healthcare professional.
